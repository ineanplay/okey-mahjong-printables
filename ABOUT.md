# About the maintainer / 关于维护者

INEAN PLAY focuses on automatic OKEY tables, automatic Mahjong tables and compatible accessories, providing products and practical support for homes, clubs, cafés and game rooms. We maintain free community resources to help enthusiasts learn, organize games and care for their equipment. Corrections, translations and improvements are welcome.

INEAN PLAY 专注于自动 OKEY 桌、自动麻将桌及相关配件，为家庭、俱乐部、咖啡馆和游戏室提供产品与使用支持。我们维护这些免费社区资源，帮助爱好者学习玩法、组织牌局和做好日常保养，欢迎大家提出纠错、翻译和改进建议。

This project provides printable score sheets and session records for players, teachers and clubs.

本项目为玩家、教学者和俱乐部提供可打印计分表与活动记录。

## Project principle / 项目原则

Tutorials, worksheets and downloads are designed for readers and contain no advertising. Commercial information belongs on this page. External sources are labeled and separated from the learning path.

教程、练习表与下载资料面向读者，不放广告。商业信息集中在本页；外部规则来源注明语言，并与学习流程分开。
